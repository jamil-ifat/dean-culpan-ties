Our Story

It began with a few ties.

And rather a lot of compliments.

Dean Culpan commissioned a knitted tie manufacturer to make something for his own wardrobe: distinctive ties with texture, bold stripes and the sort of character that makes getting dressed a pleasure.

Then people started asking where he bought them.

At first, he took the compliments. Eventually, he took the hint.

Dean Culpan Ties was born from that simplest of endorsements: people wanting what someone else was wearing.

Years in luxury hospitality have given Dean an eye for the details people remember. A well-chosen tie works much the same way as a warm welcome: it sets the tone before the conversation has properly begun.

A little old-school swagger, worn with an easy smile.

That spirit runs through The Tycoon. Rich navy, warm burnt orange and cream. Confident stripes, tactile knit and a square finish.
