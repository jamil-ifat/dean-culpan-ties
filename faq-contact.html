Questions about a tie, an order or a gift? We're friendly.

Frequently asked

What are the ties made from?

Knitted construction with a square-cut end. [Material and dimensions to be confirmed with the manufacturer.]

How long does delivery take?

UK standard is 2–4 working days. See the shipping page for full details.

Can I send a tie as a gift?

Yes. Every tie arrives gift boxed, and you can add a handwritten note at checkout.

Can I return a tie?

Within 30 days, unworn and in the original box, for a refund or exchange.

How should I care for a knitted tie?

Spot clean only. Hang or roll it; never fold it.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes: Europe and the rest of the world. Prices and timings are on the shipping page.
