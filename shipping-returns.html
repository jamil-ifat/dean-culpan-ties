Returns

Not quite right? Return unworn ties in their original box within 30 days for a refund or exchange.

  • Email us your order number to start a return.
  • Post the tie back in its box. UK returns label provided.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method within 5 working days of receipt.

Care

Spot clean only. Hang or roll to store; never fold a knitted tie.
